Why Now

Iowa's AI classroom shift is here.

Are you ready to teach it?
01

Pending Legislation

Iowa bills HSB 610, HF 2540, and SF 2094 would require students in the class of 2030-31+ to complete CS and AI coursework. Teachers need to be ready.

02

Federal Priority

The 2025 Presidential Executive Order "Advancing AI Education for American Youth" makes AI literacy a national teacher-training priority.

03

Ready-to-Use Templates

Leave with classroom-ready AI assignment templates, lesson plans, and rubrics — aligned to Iowa CS standards and ready to deploy on day one this fall.
